## The project

A student team worked with a real client on a moving-estimation workflow. The broader product vision was larger than the term allowed; the useful outcome was a working, deployable foundation with clear remaining scope.

## My contribution

As the cloud engineer, I mapped requirements to cloud services, evaluated the architecture, and recommended Azure for cost and compatibility with the client’s environment. The foundation included Static Web Apps, App Service, Azure Maps, Cosmos DB, Container Registry, a containerized Java/Spring Boot backend, and a GitHub Actions delivery path.

## What stayed with me

Requirements, cost sensitivity, architecture, and delivery are connected. Expectation-setting and scope sequencing matter just as much as the implementation.
