## The project

A four-person hackathon team built an EV route-planning prototype under tight time constraints and with limited free charging-station data. The team won the Ford EV challenge.

## My contribution

I found a public Canadian charging-station source, validated the full stack locally, created the backend Dockerfile, connected the repository to Heroku, and authored the Procfile used for the hosted backend. The stable URL gave the frontend developer a shared integration point without requiring a local backend setup. I also supported the final presentation.

## What stayed with me

The hosting itself was technically simple. Its value was in reducing dependency friction and creating leverage for the rest of the team to iterate.

> This was a team award and a collaborative product. I am not claiming ownership of the Django backend, React frontend, or a custom CI/CD pipeline.
