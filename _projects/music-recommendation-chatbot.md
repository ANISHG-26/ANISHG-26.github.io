## The project

A collaborative music-recommendation prototype combining song features with a conversational interface. The team explored the problem through shared ML, application, and integration work.

## My contribution

My contribution included participation in the ML workflow, application/API assistance, and containerized cloud deployment and integration in a personal GCP environment.

## What stayed with me

Packaging, deployment, and connecting components felt more energizing than model theory alone. Music also remained a meaningful domain for technical exploration.

> This was a collaborative project. I am not presenting sole model ownership or ownership of every connected component.
