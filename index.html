A Pattern, Then A Direction.

Build · Reflect · Improve

I learn by building. I try an idea, put it to work, and see what happens. When something breaks or creates friction, I look for the reason. Then I improve the workflow or the foundation. I followed this pattern through cloud projects, CI/CD, containerized testing, and Kubernetes delivery. Over time, it pointed me toward Platform Engineering: building systems that help other people do their work with confidence.
